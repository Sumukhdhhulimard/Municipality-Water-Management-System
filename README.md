"Municipality Water Management System"

Advanced Database Management System Project

"A full-stack Municipality Water Management System designed to digitize and streamline municipal water services, including resident registration, water connection management, billing, payments, complaint handling, water quality monitoring, incident management, and AI-assisted information retrieval."

The project demonstrates the practical integration of PostgreSQL, MongoDB, pgvector, FastAPI, and React to build a modern database-driven municipal service platform.

"Project Overview"

Traditional municipal water management systems often face challenges such as inefficient record maintenance, delayed complaint resolution, limited transparency in billing, and difficulty monitoring water distribution.

This project addresses these challenges by providing a centralized platform for residents and municipal operators.

Objectives

Digitize resident and water connection records.

Manage water bills and payment information.

Provide a complaint registration and tracking system.

Support municipal operators in managing service requests.

Monitor water quality and operational incidents.

Demonstrate relational, NoSQL, and vector database technologies.

Provide AI-assisted access to water management information.
